# Decision log

Every decision that changes how the business or this workspace runs, with its date and its
reason. Six months from now this file answers "why did we do it this way?".

## Rules

- Add new entries at the bottom. Never edit or delete an old entry.
- A changed mind is a new entry that names the date of the entry it replaces.
- One decision per entry. Use the real date (`date +%F` in the terminal).
- Claude adds an entry when you say "log that decision", and offers to when you settle
  something that will matter later.

## Entry format

```
## YYYY-MM-DD: <the decision in one line>
- Decided: <what you chose>
- Why: <the reason, in one or two sentences>
- Instead of: <what you turned down>
- Look at this again when: <a date, or the event that would change your mind>
```
